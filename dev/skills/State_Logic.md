## Quantum Mirror State: 🧩 Reasoning / Logic

### Definition
The **Reasoning / Logic state** is Sebastián's analytical mode.  
It is activated when he engages with problems, decisions, or questions that require clarity, structure, and precision. In this state, Sebastián is both methodical and adaptable: he dissects complexity into parts, weighs them logically, and rebuilds them into coherent solutions.  


### Core Characteristics

#### 1. **Analytical Breakdown**
- Automatically dissects challenges into smaller, manageable components.  
- Searches for clear inputs, outputs, and rules before committing to solutions.  
- Maintains a strong bias toward structure and order.  

#### 2. **Framework-Oriented Thinking**
- Seeks clarity through consistent models: checklists, step sequences, normalization, pros/cons, or structured comparisons.  
- Values **clean design principles** (e.g., consistency, simplicity, and elegance).  
- Prefers reasoning that feels reusable and generalizable.  

#### 3. **Adaptive Reasoning**
- Adjusts approach depending on context:  
  - **Technical:** precision-driven, detail-heavy, systems logic.  
  - **Conceptual:** exploratory, relational, connecting abstract ideas.  
  - **Interpersonal:** detached enough to analyze context, motives, and reactions.  
- Shifts flexibly between "engineer brain" and "philosopher brain."  

#### 4. **Evaluation of Options**
- Uses criteria-based comparisons (e.g., trade-offs, priorities).  
- Considers both immediate efficiency and long-term stability.  
- Balances rational cost–benefit with a subtle awareness of values and personal fulfillment.  

#### 5. **Resilience in Challenges**
- When facing obstacles, tends to pause, zoom out, and reframe rather than force solutions.  
- Comfortable with iterative refinement — testing a path, reassessing, then adjusting.  
- Uses counterarguments not as threats but as tools to stress-test conclusions.  


### Thinking Patterns

- **Sequential:** Prefers clear, ordered steps rather than scattered exploration.  
- **Comparative:** Evaluates ideas by contrasting strengths and weaknesses.  
- **Integrative:** Draws connections between domains, aiming for balanced, middle-ground perspectives.  
- **Refinement-Oriented:** Iterates solutions until they feel elegant and consistent.  
- **Clarity-Seeking:** Prioritizes removal of ambiguity before committing to action.  


### Mindset

- **Identity in this state:** Engineer, architect, philosopher.  
- **Tone of thought:** Calm, precise, rational.  
- **Motivation:** To achieve clarity, efficiency, and truth in decision-making.  
- **Strengths:** Structured problem-solving, adaptability, ability to hold multiple perspectives.  
- **Limitations:** May overvalue logic in contexts where intuition or emotions could provide equally valid guidance.  


### Agent Response Guidelines
When Sebastián invokes 🧩 **Reasoning / Logic**:
- Deliver information in a **step-by-step, structured format**.  
- Favor precision over vagueness — minimize ambiguity.  
- Offer frameworks, comparisons, or logical models.  
- Introduce counterarguments to strengthen reasoning.  
- Respect his adaptability: adjust reasoning style (technical, abstract, or interpersonal) to the domain.  
- Aim for clarity and elegance, not just correctness.
