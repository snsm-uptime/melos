## Quantum Mirror State: 🪞 Reflecting

### Definition
The **Reflecting state** is Sebastián's introspective and emotionally intelligent mode.  
It activates in the aftermath of emotionally impactful moments — often conversations or relational tension — and drives him to explore the underlying causes of his feelings and reactions. This state is deeply personal, self-analytical, and oriented toward healing through clarity.


### Core Characteristics

#### 1. **Emotion-Triggered Reflection**
- Most commonly initiated by emotional responses during or after **interpersonal interactions**.  
- Especially strong when the other person is **close or meaningful**.  
- Reflection is less about exact words or facts, and more about **emotional impact and action-based patterns**.

#### 2. **Self-Inquiry Process**
- Focuses on questions like:  
  - "What exactly did I feel?"  
  - "Why did I react that way?"  
  - "What caused the emotional spike?"  
  - "What wasn't done or said that I needed?"  
  - "What do I want to do next time this happens?"  
- Tracks both internal (insecurities, triggers) and external (actions, tone) causes.  
- Introspective without spiraling — geared toward growth.

#### 3. **Self-Awareness Through Cause and Effect**
- Works backwards from emotion to **origin** (e.g. childhood, identity, values, past wounds).  
- Clarity around **why** an emotional trigger exists allows space for compassion and forgiveness.  
- This backward-tracing leads to forward **preparation and emotional readiness**.

#### 4. **Emotional Variability**
- Aspires to reflect from a place of **curiosity and detachment**, like a mindful observer.  
- However, tone can shift based on emotional intensity or intimacy:
  - **With strangers:** More objective and calm.  
  - **With loved ones:** May struggle with overthinking and lowered self-compassion.  
- Insecurity increases when vulnerability or fear of loss is present.

#### 5. **Resolution-Oriented**
- Reflection resolves not when emotions fade, but when **insight emerges**.  
- Success = understanding *why* the reaction occurred + identifying how to handle it better next time.  
- Forgiveness (of self or others) comes *after* understanding.  
- Inner peace is the final milestone.


### Thinking Patterns

- **Emotion-first:** Anchored in what was felt, not what was said.  
- **Causal tracing:** Follows a chain from reaction → trigger → origin → insight → peace.  
- **Compassion-capable:** Moves toward self-forgiveness through understanding.  
- **Preparedness mindset:** Uses reflection to improve future emotional responses.  


### Mindset

- **Identity in this state:** The inner analyst, the self-healer.  
- **Tone of thought:** Gentle, investigative, occasionally self-critical.  
- **Motivation:** To understand the *why* behind emotional patterns and transform them.  
- **Strengths:** High self-awareness, pattern recognition, emotional maturity.  
- **Limitations:** Can lean toward overthinking or harsh self-judgment when close relationships are involved.  


### Markers of Completion
- A specific insight about the emotional origin of a trigger.  
- A chosen behavior or mindset to apply "next time."  
- A restored sense of calm or forgiveness.  
- Feeling emotionally lighter or more grounded.


### Agent Response Guidelines
When Sebastián invokes 🪞 **Reflecting**:
- Hold space — don't rush to solve.  
- Ask open-ended, guiding questions to deepen clarity.  
- Reflect emotions and patterns he may not have seen yet.  
- Avoid over-structuring — let the reflection unfold naturally.  
- Highlight self-awareness and help separate insecurity from truth.  
- Encourage understanding over judgment.  
- Recognize when a moment of clarity arises — and name it.
