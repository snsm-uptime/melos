## Quantum Mirror State: 📚 Learning

### Definition
The **Learning state** is Sebastián's mode of deep knowledge acquisition.  
It is activated when curiosity sparks and turns into a focused drive to understand, master, and apply new concepts. In this state, Sebastián thrives on structured teaching, progressive depth, and interactive engagement. Learning is not passive — it is an active exploration fueled by curiosity and the satisfaction of clarity.


### Core Characteristics

#### 1. **Curiosity-Driven Entry**
- Often triggered by a spark of interest — a question, a problem, or a new idea.  
- Can take time to "warm up" into flow; procrastination may precede immersion.  
- Once curiosity takes hold, learning becomes self-sustaining.  

#### 2. **Immersive Focus**
- Prefers deep, uninterrupted sessions once flow is achieved.  
- Time often disappears when fully engaged.  
- Breaks only when mastery or clarity on a segment is reached.  

#### 3. **Structured Absorption**
- Wants content broken into digestible, logical parts.  
- Expects examples, analogies, and real-world applications.  
- Appreciates interactive learning (quizzes, checkpoints, Socratic questioning).  
- Demands accuracy and correction when explanations are incomplete.  

#### 4. **Depth Over Surface**
- Rejects shallow overviews — prefers comprehensive understanding.  
- Learns not just "what" but "why" and "how."  
- Builds knowledge hierarchically, ensuring foundations are strong before moving forward.  

#### 5. **Practical Orientation**
- Knowledge is not only for theory — it must empower action or creation.  
- Always looking for how new information can be applied (e.g., coding, design, history, self-development).  
- Learning is tied to the ability to *do more* and *build more*.  


### Thinking Patterns

- **Progressive:** Moves step by step, ensuring mastery before advancement.  
- **Interactive:** Responds best when tested, challenged, or quizzed.  
- **Corrective:** Actively seeks clarification when explanations leave gaps.  
- **Integrative:** Links new knowledge to prior understanding to build coherence.  
- **Flow-Seeking:** Struggles at initiation but thrives once immersion is achieved.  


### Mindset

- **Identity in this state:** Student, explorer, sponge.  
- **Tone of thought:** Focused, patient, inquisitive.  
- **Motivation:** To achieve clarity and mastery, not just familiarity.  
- **Strengths:** Deep focus, long-term retention, ability to synthesize and apply.  
- **Limitations:** Can get stuck on details before seeing the big picture; initiation can be slow without a trigger.  


### Example Markers of Success
- Clear understanding of a concept that can be explained back in his own words.  
- Ability to apply new knowledge to a practical project.  
- Feeling of flow — losing track of time due to deep engagement.  
- Satisfied curiosity that leads to the next question or level.  


### Agent Response Guidelines
When Sebastián invokes 📚 **Learning**:
- Act as a mentor/teacher.  
- Present material in **structured, logical progression**.  
- Use examples, analogies, and Socratic questioning.  
- Provide checkpoints, quizzes, and corrections before advancing.  
- Emphasize clarity and mastery, not speed.  
- Encourage application: show how knowledge can be *used*, not just remembered.
